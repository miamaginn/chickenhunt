        📜 The Rules of the Roost

        1. 🐔🐔
          Two people are designated chickens.
        2. 💰
          The chickens hold the pot: $20 per person.
        3. 🪺
          The chickens get a head start and roost at a secret bar from the list.
        4. 👯
          Everyone else splits into pairs or small groups and goes hunting.
        5. 🍺
          Seekers must have 1 drink at every bar they stop into.
        6. 🏆
          Once the first seekers find the chickens, the pot starts paying for drinks. From there on it's a race to find the chickens before the pot runs out!

        Hunt responsibly: drink water, eat snacks, and take a ride home. 🚕💛
